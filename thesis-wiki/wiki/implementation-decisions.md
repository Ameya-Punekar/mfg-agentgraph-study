## How to use this log

Add an entry when a change materially affects a V1 requirement, pipeline stage, external interface, validation/failure contract, evidence package, evaluation comparability, or deployment assumption. Routine refactors that preserve behavior do not need an entry.

Each entry must state:

- **Date and status**
- **Decision or deviation**
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**
- **Context**
- **Chosen option and alternatives rejected**
- **Consequences and risks**
- **Evidence**: tests, command, benchmark, or pull request

Never edit the submitted Architecture V1 page merely to hide a divergence. Record the divergence here and create a later architecture version if the baseline itself changes.

## 2026-11-01 — Fuseki provenance persistence and Stage 10 callback proof

- **Status**: Accepted locally for Issue #31 M4; final live proof and full gate pending.
- **Decision or deviation**: Reuse the existing `FusekiProvenanceStore.persist` bound method unchanged as the `PersistTrace` callback for Stage 10. Add an opt-in live proof that exercises one exact per-run named graph, deterministic canonical update, exact readback, and test-owned exact cleanup. This is not an Architecture V1 deviation and adds no dependency.
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**: Architecture V1 R5 and S4 Stage 10; primary RQ3. Deployment evidence supports R1/RQ4, while RQ1 is limited to reproducible strategy evidence rather than comparison results.
- **Context**: The adapter already performs bounded canonicalization, loopback validation, one named-graph update, and exact readback. M4 therefore closes the integration contract at the existing callback injection point rather than changing the orchestrator, defaults, or signatures.
- **Chosen option and alternatives rejected**: Keep the demo default in-memory; use a separate SHA-256-derived graph per provenance ID; require exact readback before exposing both stable IDs; preserve uncertain external state as `DISPATCHED_UNKNOWN`; and keep production free of cleanup, retry, and rollback. Reject orchestrator changes, default service discovery, wildcard/default-graph cleanup, and claims of atomicity, privacy, encryption, safety, optimality, or durability beyond confirmed exact readback.
- **Consequences and risks**: Update failures after dispatch may leave none, some, or all triples, so Stage 10 continues to fail closed with sanitized `PROVENANCE_FAILED_CLOSED` and no IDs/evidence. Hash-derived graph names provide isolation/pseudonymization, not encryption or privacy. Retention, access, authentication, and operational deployment assumptions remain external responsibilities.
- **Evidence**: Stage 10 callback tests use the actual `store.persist` bound method and cover successful update/readback plus update/readback failure release blocking. `tests/integration/live_fuseki_provenance.py` is non-default-collected and requires dedicated `MFG_AGENTGRAPH_FUSEKI_PROVENANCE_*` variables; it has not been run here, so no live proof is claimed. M1–M3 implementation/reviews and the controller full gate are prior evidence; the M4 full gate remains pending.

## 2026-09-08 — Deterministic fail-safe entity resolver

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Decision or deviation**: Add `mfg_agentgraph.entity_resolution` as a dependency-free deterministic resolver for known manufacturing assets. This is not an Architecture V1 deviation.
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**: Architecture V1 R2 structured domain grounding; S4 stage 1 entity and asset resolution before structured retrieval; supports RQ2 by making ontology-bound asset selection explicit and fail-safe.
- **Context**: S4 needs a narrow boundary that turns a caller-provided asset hint or already-extracted term into one known asset before later KG lookup, candidate generation, RDF mapping, SHACL validation, and provenance stages can safely use that identity.

Multiple plausible matches and no/low-confidence matches require operator disambiguation rather than automatic selection.
- **Evidence**: 44 focused entity-resolution tests; original missing-module RED; review-driven canonical-ID-whitespace RED/fix; independent local specification and code-quality reviews reported no blocking issues before the initial product commit. - **Status**: Accepted for Issue #115 at the owner's direction to finish the issue. Adds a superseding release version; v1 and v2 stay byte-identical. Records one **unmet protocol clause** (meaningful 10^4-triple graph) and one **approximately met** clause (document count).
- **Decision or deviation**: (1) v3 keeps v2's documents/chunks/assets/sensors/observations, adds ten verified families from `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v3/sources-v3.json`, and replaces only the authored layer. (2) **Evidence-chain enforcement moved into code**: `load_authored_v3` rejects a non-trap query that omits a procedure or a supporting chunk, a chunk the cited procedure does not claim, a claim whose quote is not a verbatim substring of its chunk, and an unknown asset; the rule is now a build failure rather than reviewer discipline. (3) Eleven claims the corpus could not support were dropped or narrowed (fan inlet/outlet inspection, fan system-effect ordering, pump cavitation/recirculation and trapped-air noise, pump isolation before impeller work, valve gland inspection and depressurisation, valve stroke verification, slider guide cleaning, carriage-play verification, and `atq-201`'s observation time, which the contract cannot express). (4) `ScenarioCorpus.provenance_profile` is an **optional** release field (`legacy` default, `complete` for v3); `complete` publishes document `sourceUri`/`sourceSha256`/`relevanceTier`/`textLayer`/`allowsRedistribution`/`textSha256`/extraction identity and recording `datasetDoi`/`recordingCrc32`/`recordingSeconds`/`sampleRateHz`/`channels`/`bitsPerSample`, so a SPARQL consumer can follow a chunk to its source artifact and a recording to its dataset identity. (5) Versioned builders now **refuse a base that is not the frozen release they claim** (`_FROZEN_V1_DIGEST`, `_FROZEN_V2_DIGEST`), and the v1 writer refuses to replace a divergent frozen artifact instead of overwriting it.
- **Defects fixed in the same change** (each found by review or by running the loader, each with a regression test): (a) `_MAX_BODY_BYTES = 1_048_576` truncated the corpus CONSTRUCT response (measured 1,067,231 bytes for v1) into a turtle syntax error, so **no** corpus load could succeed; the bound is now 64 MiB and an over-limit body raises instead of truncating. (b) `corpus load` ran the general bootstrap and therefore also created/replaced the stack's **shapes graph**, contradicting its documented single-graph guarantee; `bootstrap_stack(..., include_shapes=False)` and the vestigial `--shapes` option is gone. (c) `CorpusChunk.text_sha256` was validated for syntax only, so edited text could load under an old digest; it is now compared with the text. (d) `--version v1` overwrote frozen v1 artifacts unconditionally. (e) A bare DOI serialised as a `file:///…` URIRef; it is now a literal and `_http_uri` refuses any non-absolute URL before serialisation.
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**: R2 and R6; RQ1/RQ2 directly, RQ3 provenance and RQ4 reproducible loading as prerequisites. No runtime dependency added. `StackConfig` now rejects two graph roles sharing one named graph, which is a configuration-contract tightening, not an Architecture V1 deviation.
- **Context**: Independent review found that v2's rubrics could not be satisfied by v2's own evidence, that every procedure cited the first three chunks of its document regardless of entailment, and that the observation layer reached its triple count by repetition. Separately, running the documented load command failed outright, which no reading of the code would have revealed.
- **Chosen option and alternatives rejected**: Rebuild the authored layer on evidence that actually entails it and add the ten licensable families, keeping the observation layer unchanged by owner decision. Rejected: relaxing the chunk rule to admit non-procedural text; editing v1/v2 in place; counting the two USBR manuals whose own statement limits them to internal Bureau use; claiming 50 families or Track A scale; inventing the eleven unsupported claims; keeping `--shapes` for compatibility.
- **Consequences and risks**: v3 has **49 artifacts / 48 families, 528 chunks, 1,612 observations, 14,761 triples**; slider coverage rises 3→7 and five documents now carry share-alike obligations. **Documents are approximately met at 48**; the **graph is not**: 9,600 of 14,761 triples are 1,600 duplicated `nominal_*` observations with no value, time or state, so stripping them leaves ~5,100 triples and no Track A scale claim exists until a successor adds authored `value`/`observed-at`/state fields. A claim quoted from `doc:nrc-nureg-cr-6478` is machine OCR and must be checked against the page image; two documents are HTML captures whose text is only as stable as the committed capture.
- **Evidence**: `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v3/{corpus-v3.json,corpus-v3.trig,sources-v3.json,authored-v3.json,dataset-card-v3.md}`; release digest `sha256:0c11ebe5aa1ea4d6ef55f9785714e58476391d01759d7e4efe6d017ad2f210fe`, development digest `8d170b8fcec51e8442da7dd44ae0740e0e7b97710586d88f0db2214079fb1f82`. `--version v1 --verify` reproduces `16a9568…`, `--version v2 --verify` reproduces `91f4894…`, and a **cold-cache** v2 run (`--cache-dir /tmp/sc-cold-check`, empty beforehand) reproduced `91f4894…`. Live load on the pinned stack: divergent v3 load refused, `--recreate` installed 13,779→(final) 14,745 triples before the derivation records, 14,761 after, re-load reported `already_present`, and factory (18) / provenance (0) / shapes (103) were unchanged. ## 2026-11-13 — Derived sources carry a structured derivation record, and v3 is re-frozen

- **Status**: Accepted for Issue #115 after a second independent review, before integration. Supersedes the `0d732475…` digest of the entry above; v1 and v2 remain byte-identical. Contract expansion is additive and optional, so the contract identifier is unchanged.
- **Decision or deviation**: (1) `CorpusDocument` gains an optional `derivation: SourceDerivation` (`kind` ∈ {`ocr_text_layer`, `assembled_capture`}, `origin_uri`, `origin_sha256`, `manifest`, `entry`, optional `derived_artifact_sha256`). It is serialised as the optional document key `derivation`, refused on any release whose `provenance_profile` is not `complete`, and exported to TriG as `derivationKind`, `originUri`, `originSha256`, `derivationManifest`, `derivationEntry`, `derivedArtifactSha256`. (2) `source_uri`/`source_sha256` keep their existing meaning — the official locator plus the digest of the artifact the release extracted from — so the frozen v2 record and digest do not change; the origin linkage is carried by `derivation` instead. (3) `CorpusProcedure` now rejects any lineage other than `authored_curated`, mirroring assets and sensors. (4) `load_authored_v3` requires every non-trap query to cite a declared observation whose asset the cited procedure covers. (5) Two HTML captures build from committed `*.visible-text.txt` snapshots declared in `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v3-derived/derived-inputs-v3.json`, with per-component page URLs, order and segment digests.
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**: R2 (structured domain grounding) and R6 (comparability unit); RQ3 provenance directly, RQ1/RQ2 as prerequisites. No strategy, SHACL, repair, metric or fixture behaviour changed. No runtime dependency added.
- **Context**: Review established two consumer-visible falsehoods in the release as first drafted. (a) The graph published `sourceUri` = the NRC ML18086B223 URL beside `sourceSha256` = `98bb723e…`, the hash of a locally OCR'd PDF that no URL serves, and it published capture hashes for two HTML bundles whose declared download URLs return different bytes. A SPARQL consumer following the graph would download bytes that do not match. (b) Neither v3's cold-cache reproduction nor the documented evidence chain held for those documents: a fresh `--cache-dir` build failed at FarmBot (HTTP 404) and PrintNC (single page). A third finding was that a procedure could declare `mimii_derived` lineage and a non-trap query could omit its observation entirely, contradicting the card.
- **Chosen option and alternatives rejected**: An optional structured `derivation` object, emitted only for `complete` releases, with the origin/derived/text chain resolvable per predicate. Rejected: rewriting `source_uri`/`source_sha256` for CR-6478 (it would change frozen v2 bytes and discard the official NRC identity); packing the chain into `ExtractionRecord.rule` as opaque JSON (the public model could not validate or query it, and the field's contract is an extraction rule); shipping v3 unchanged with the caveat in prose only; committing the captured HTML (redistributes website code and is unnecessary for text extraction).
- **Consequences and risks**: v3 is re-frozen at **14,761 triples** (16 derivation triples) with release digest `0c11ebe5…`; composition is otherwise unchanged at 49 artifacts / 48 families, 528 chunks, 1,612 observations. `corpus-v3.trig` gained exactly the 16 derivation triples. The derivation record states provenance, not reproducibility of the underlying capture: two of the three origins are snapshots whose re-capture would produce different text, which the card records. A v1/v2 rebuild cannot acquire a derivation, because the freeze check precedes it. The scale position from the entry above is unchanged — 9,600 of 14,761 triples remain multiplicity — so still no Track A scale claim.
- **Evidence**: `--version v3 --verify` and `--version v2 --verify` pass (`0c11ebe5…`, `91f4894…`); a genuinely empty cache (`rm -rf /tmp/corpus-cold-v3`, then `--version v3 --verify --cache-dir /tmp/corpus-cold-v3`) reproduced `0c11ebe5…`, downloading the eight PDFs and reading the two captures from the committed snapshots; a 1-byte-tampered capture fails closed. `tests/test_corpus_release.py` covers the derivation predicates, the legacy-profile refusal, the `entry` binding and the manifest path rule, plus the procedure-lineage rejection; `tests/test_corpus_builder.py` covers a missing, undeclared and uncovered-asset observation. `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v3-derived/derived-inputs-v3.json` (11,389 B) declares both captures.

## 2026-11-13 — Observations must state something: corpus v4 replaces identifier-only records

- **Status**: Accepted for Issue #137 at the owner's instruction to fix the limitations recorded when #115 was closed. Adds a superseding release version; v1, v2 and v3 stay byte-identical.
- **Decision or deviation**: (1) `CorpusObservation` gained optional `value`, `observed_at` and `operating_state`, serialised only when present so frozen releases keep their canonical bytes. (2) A value must be a finite number **and** declare a sensor (the sensor carries the unit) **and** declare an operating state from the closed vocabulary `baseline | transient | anomaly | recovery`; a timestamp must be an unambiguous UTC instant; a `mimii_derived` event may declare neither a value nor a timestamp; a reading must be made by a sensor hosted on the same asset. (3) `ScenarioCorpus.identifier_only_observation_ids` reports records that state no reading, event or time, `mfg-agentgraph-corpus verify` prints the count, and the v4 build refuses to publish a non-empty list. The rule is deliberately **not** in the contract, because v1–v3 are frozen with 1,608 such records and must keep loading. (4) A release that states readings must publish `provenance_profile: complete`, and TriG exports `corpus:value`, `corpus:observedAt` and `corpus:operatingState`. (5) v4 copies v3's documents, chunks, assets, sensors, procedures and queries verbatim behind a `_FROZEN_V3_DIGEST` check, which pins canonical release content rather than file bytes (a reformatted base is the same release; an edited field is not) and replaces only the observation layer with a seedless deterministic scenario timeline declared in `_PROPERTY_PROFILE`.
- **Affected V1 requirement/stage**: R2 (SSN/SOSA-aligned domain grounding) and R6 (comparability unit); RQ1/RQ2 directly, RQ3 provenance and RQ4 reproducible loading as prerequisites. No Architecture V1 deviation: the record gains optional fields and no pipeline stage, metric, shape or contract version changes.
- **Context**: The v3 card recorded that 1,608 of 1,612 observations carried no value, no time and no state, so 9,640 of 14,761 triples came from 1,600 nominal records and eight sensorless context records that differed only by identifier, and the protocol's meaningful 10^4-triple clause stayed unmet. That was a correctness problem rather than a cosmetic one: a record that states nothing cannot support a claim, and a triple count earned that way misrepresents the corpus.
- **Chosen option and alternatives rejected**: State a reading, an instant and a state for every authored observation, keep MIMII events value- and time-free, and make the identifier-only count a publish gate. Rejected: keeping the 25 identical records (the defect itself); generating random noise per record (unreproducible without a committed seed and still uninformative); attaching MIMII recordings to invented wall-clock times (fabricated evidence); chasing 10^5 triples by widening the series without new information; putting the refusal in the contract (it would reject the frozen releases).
- **Consequences and risks**: v4 has **49 artifacts / 48 families, 528 chunks, 8 assets, 64 sensors, 1,604 observations (1,600 readings + 4 MIMII events), 4 procedures, 10 development queries and 19,521 triples**, with **zero** identifier-only observations. Of the triples, 14,400 are the readings (9 predicates each), 60 the MIMII events, and 5,061 the documents, chunks, sensors, assets, procedures and queries. **The values are synthetic fixtures**: the profile encodes no manufacturer limit, diagnostic threshold, inspection interval or measured behaviour, and `anomaly` names a scenario window, not a fault verdict. The protocol's triple-order clause is now met by informative records, which is a scale statement only — no topical-breadth, realism, decision-quality or Track A claim follows, and independent gold labels remain unmet (#132, #126). The development digest changes from v3 because the release version is part of the development-split document, not because a query changed.
- **Evidence**: `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v4/{corpus-v4.json,corpus-v4.trig,dataset-card-v4.md}`; release digest `sha256:218300e2b5b6515493f09e30b309d451d72b8b9b145a9949f6e5e4a6548e6d89`, development digest `a8966fa9a30665fa19020351f2b883cf9e21f6e5b4bb0070c21809a613ad4af6`. `--version v4 --verify` passes, and `--version {v1,v2,v3} --verify` still reproduce `16a9568…`, `91f4894…` and `0c11ebe5…`. `tests/test_corpus_release.py` covers every rejection path (value without sensor, non-finite value, non-UTC timestamp, unknown state, unstated state, MIMII value or time, legacy profile with readings, sensor hosted by a different asset), the identifier-only predicate and the published reading chain; `tests/test_corpus_builder.py` covers generator determinism, per-sensor uniqueness, profile bounds, the presence of every operating state and the publish guard.
