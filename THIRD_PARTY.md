# Third-party material and licensing

The retrieval corpus under `evaluation/scenario-corpus-v*/` bundles short, source-located excerpts of 49 third-party documents (528 chunks), plus three pinned derived-text rebuild inputs named below. Every excerpt keeps its own licence. The repository's own notice does not, and cannot, relicense third-party text.

The "no licence granted" reservation in `README.md` applies to material the authors created — the code, tests, and author-written documentation and scenario data. **It does not apply to, and must not be read as adding conditions to, the third-party excerpts or the derived text under `evaluation/`.** Those remain licensed by their rights holders on the terms recorded here. A redistributor of a corpus release must keep this file and comply with each upstream term; the authors' own reservation cannot and does not override them.

**What this means in practice:** you may read this repository, and the excerpts remain available under the terms in the table below. If you want to reuse an excerpt, its licence — not this repository's terms — governs that reuse. Share-alike and non-commercial conditions still attach, and attribution is required where the table says so.

## Authoring, mixing, and the share-alike boundary

Five documents are share-alike: `doc:lulzbot-taz6-manual`, `doc:ose-universal-cnc-axis`, `doc:reprap-prusa-mendel`, `doc:v1e-lowrider`, `doc:voron-2.4r2-assembly-manual`. Each chunk is stored as a contiguous quoted excerpt with its source document, line range and `text_sha256`, so an excerpt can be traced to its origin and removed without touching the others. Where the corpus synthesises rather than quotes — observations, sensors, assets, procedures, queries — the material is authored and contains no third-party text.

`doc:practica-rope-pump-2011` carries a free-of-charge, not-for-profit condition with a written-permission requirement for commercial reproduction; the corpus records it as `practica_free_copy_noncommercial`.

`doc:lulzbot-taz6-manual` prints "Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International" and parenthesises "CC BY-SA 4.0". Either reading permits redistribution; the conservative share-alike reading is recorded and true is asserted for `share_alike` until the ambiguity is resolved at source.

`doc:v1e-lowrider` states CC BY-SA on the site and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on machine pages, so the conservative non-commercial share-alike reading applies.
