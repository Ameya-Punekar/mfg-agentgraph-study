# Large Language Models for Manufacturing (2024)

**Summary**: A 2024 survey by Li et al. exploring the integration and potential applications of LLMs within the manufacturing domain, including quality control, engineering design, robotics, and knowledge management.

**Sources**: `raw/converted/LLMs_for_Manufacturing_Survey_2024.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-11.

**Thesis relevance**: RQ3 / RQ4 — manufacturing-domain context for operator-facing anomaly triage and locally deployable decision-support agents.

---

## Key Takeaways

The survey explores the transformative potential of LLMs in manufacturing, highlighting their strengths in text processing, data analysis, and zero-shot learning. 

Key applications identified include:
- **Quality Control & Cost Control**
- **Supply Chain Management**
- **Engineering Design & Robotics Engineering**
- **Knowledge & Patent Management**

The survey emphasizes that LLMs currently excel in supporting roles rather than direct computational or high-precision design roles, making them ideal for knowledge retrieval and triage workflows.

## Relationship to the Thesis

- **Anomaly Triage Context**: The thesis's primary scenario—using an LLM agent to triage pump station anomalies based on Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)—is perfectly aligned with the survey's findings. The LLM acts as an orchestrator and knowledge retrieval support system (via the KG) rather than attempting raw numerical computation.
- **Industry 4.0/5.0 Shift**: Frames the thesis's contribution within the broader post-pandemic shift toward smart manufacturing, knowledge management, and AI-driven resilience.
