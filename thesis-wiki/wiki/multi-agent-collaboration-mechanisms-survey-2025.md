## Key Takeaways

The survey characterizes collaborations between LLM-based agents based on several key dimensions:
- **Actors**: The agents involved in the collaboration.
- **Type**: The nature of the collaboration, spanning cooperation, competition, or coopetition.
- **Structure**: The communication and organizational structure (e.g., peer-to-peer, centralized, distributed).
- **Strategy**: The overarching approach to collaboration (e.g., role-based, rule-based, model-based).

## Relationship to the Thesis

- **Single-Agent vs Multi-Agent**: While this survey details sophisticated multi-agent collaboration mechanisms, the thesis explicitly opts for a single-agent framework (MFG-AgentGraph) driven by a SHACL self-correction loop due to efficiency and latency constraints. The survey serves as contextual background on the alternative multi-agent scaling path that the thesis consciously avoids.
